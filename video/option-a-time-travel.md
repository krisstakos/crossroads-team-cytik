# Option A: "Where You Started" (time travel), 2:00

**Pitch:** A notification asks fit, happy Alex *"Do you remember where you started?"* and pulls him three years back into the same apartment, the same sofa, the same night. He watches his past self make the exact choice that changed everything, and he cannot stop heckling it.
**Tone:** Warm, playful, a little sentimental. Present Alex is a ghost in the room: he can talk, nobody can hear him, and he knows every line by heart.
**Why this version works:** the comedy comes from a man watching himself, not from how he looked. Past Alex differs only by tired eyes, a hoodie and a phone at 4%. One actor, no prosthetics.

Shared rules, cast, screens, in-app copy and motion spec live in `VIDEO-SCRIPT.md`.

## Look

- **Present:** golden hour, saturated, the R&B groove.
- **Past:** the same apartment, cold and slightly underexposed, no music, only room tone. Present Alex stands in it still lit warm gold, so the two grades share every frame.

## Differences from the first draft

- The notification names *Free visit at IronWorks Gym*, so the flashback starts from the very task it asks about. The first draft asked about "Walk 10 minutes" and then showed a gym visit.
- The stakes ($10, one hour) arrive at 0:56, not 1:02, and the missed-day gag lives in the realistic option instead.
- The pigeon and toddler gags are gone, because both were jokes about being slow. The replacement is the ghost of future Alex.
- No prosthetics and no second actor.
