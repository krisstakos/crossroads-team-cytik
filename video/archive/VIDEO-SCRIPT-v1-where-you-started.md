# "Where You Started": 2:00 ad for Commit.

**Tagline:** *Put your money where your goals are.*
**Tone:** Playful. Warm comedy, never mean. Past Alex is a lovable underdog, and the jokes are on the situation (the stairs, the sofa, a pigeon), not on his body.
**Core idea:** Commit doesn't nag. It remembers where you started, then bets on your next step.
**Sport:** Fitness / gym. The final task is a real one from the app: a **Gym visit** at *Ironworks Fitness*.

The app is **Commit.** (with the green dot). Every phone insert uses real screens, saved in `video/screens/`.

## Look and sound rules

- **Present:** warm golden hour, saturated, bouncy upbeat track (ukulele/handclaps).
- **Past:** cold, desaturated, slightly underexposed, **no music**, only breathing, room tone and comedic foley (squeaks, a sad trombone-free "sigh").
- **The chime:** one soft two-note tone, the same each time. It plays 3 times: notification, snap back, final notification.
- **Cast:** one actor with prosthetics, or two matched actors. Name: ALEX.

## Avoid

- Mocking the body. The joke is the situation, and Alex is always the hero
- Real charity logos. The in-app charities are sample names, so keep the turtle and branding generic
- Weight-loss claims. Say "start", not "lose"
