# Option B: "Starting Monday" (realistic), 2:00

**Tagline:** *Put your money where your goals are.*
**Tone:** Warm observational comedy. Real people saying the things people actually say. The jokes are about the lie we all tell ("I'll start Monday"), never about anyone's body.
**Core idea:** Everyone starts Monday. Commit makes you start tonight, because tonight it costs ten dollars.
**Sport:** Fitness. The task is a real one from the app: a **Free visit at IronWorks Gym** with a **$10** stake.
Shared rules, screens, in-app copy and the motion spec live in `VIDEO-SCRIPT.md`.

**Runtime logic:** The stake is a real one-hour timer, so the whole story happens in one evening plus one dawn. No time travel, no prosthetics, one actor.

| Role | Notes |
